- Bundled third-party components: see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- Emulators are **not** bundled. They are downloaded on request from their official sources
  and remain under their own licenses (PCSX2 GPL-3.0, Dolphin GPL-2.0-or-later, …).
  OmniDeck only writes configuration files for them and starts them as separate processes.
- No code was copied from EmuDeck (GPL-3.0) or any emulator. Upstream sources were read to
  verify command-line options, configuration keys and log messages; these facts are recorded
  in each manifest's `assumptions`.

## Content

OmniDeck does not bundle, download or distribute games, BIOS images, firmware, or encryption
keys, does not circumvent DRM, and does not scrape ROM sites. Users supply content they
legally own; OmniDeck organises and validates it.
